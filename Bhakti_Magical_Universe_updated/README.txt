BHAKTI'S MAGICAL UNIVERSE — Birthday Website

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the Bhakti_Magical_Universe folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It opens in Chrome or another browser.
4. Click Music on to hear the original gentle synthesized melody.
5. Open the Special portrait page, tap gallery photos, open the surprise letter, blow the candle, and celebrate!

FILES
- index.html: website structure
- style.css: design and animations
- script.js: gallery, music, letter, cake and confetti interactions
- assets/: seven gallery photos plus Bhakti’s full-page portrait collage

NOTES
- Internet is needed only for the decorative Google Fonts. The website and photos work locally.
- The music is generated by the browser; no copyrighted song is included.
- To publish online, upload the contents of this folder to a GitHub repository and enable GitHub Pages.
